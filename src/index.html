A preserved copy of a web site that stopped being updated in 2005. Nothing here is live — the band is not playing, no address is monitored, and nothing is for sale. How this was recovered

Ludington, Michigan · 2004–2005

Lost in Sanity were two sixteen-year-olds with a drum kit, a Washburn, and a copy of the recording software that came with the V-Drums. In seven months they wrote a record, changed their name to dodge a band from the eighties, posted twenty-one news updates, and put the whole thing on the internet.

The site went quiet in early 2005. Everything below survived.

From the home page

What Happened to THEM?

Our band used to be called THEM. However, there were some naming conflicts that occurred. There already was a band with that name and actually had made it decently big back in the 80’s. So, to avoid any legal difficulties we changed our name to Lost in Sanity.

Also from the home page: Lost in Sanity, a new band in the Ludington, Michigan area working hard to become noticed. Starting this past summer (2004), they have already finished a demo CD and are working to get a complete album finished. There have been many hard times along the way (for example, they had to change the band name from THEM to Lost in Sanity due to naming conflicts, but they have managed so far).

Two of them

The band

Josh Mogerman

Vocals and drums · junior in highschool

On the band forming: “I’m really excited for Lost In Sanity and hope that we can become successful in Ludington. Eventually I hope we can get some gigs, who else knows what could happen?”

On his drum lines: “From Jon Bonham (Led Zepplin), Keith Moon (The Who), and Travis Barker (blink-182).”

On his lyrics: “I write from the heart on some of the songs; of past and current experiences. Also, a lot of the lyrics come from me just wanting to go out and have fun, for example, our new song (Hangover Blues).”

On how long: “Both of them I have been doing for over 6 years.”

Brandon Martinez

Guitar · junior in highschool

On the band forming: “I think that it’s pretty awesome. This is actually the first band I have ever been in and I’ve been having a lot of fun it.”

On writing guitar parts: “Mainly, it is just a lot of messing around. However, I do tend to follow some simple scale and chord progressions. Also, Josh tends to give some pretty kickin’ rifts and I turn it into guitar.”

On inspiration: “Mostly, I get a lot of inspiration from Billie Joe Armstrong (Green Day), he turns guitar into a rhythmic, simple, yet effective instrument. He is my main source of inspiration. I also take a lot of inspiration from The Offspring, Static-X, Nickelback, and a few other bands.”

On how long: “It’s been a little over a year now. I started playing in the October of sophomore year.”

The story of this page

Lost in Sanity never owned a domain. The site lived as a folder on the hosting account of Geeky Computer Services, the guitarist’s own business — which is why every page on the original site is stamped “Copyright © 2004 Lost In Sanity & Geeky Computer Services.” When that hosting was cancelled in March 2005, the site went with it. There is no copy in the Internet Archive.

For twenty years the only trace was the name lostinsanity, which turned out to be a PureVolume and GarageBand.com username registered on January 2, 2007 — long after the band had stopped. The domain lostinsanity.net was never theirs. It was registered in 2026, specifically to give this archive somewhere to live.

What survived was the site folder itself, zipped and carried forward across two decades of machines, and the audio — sitting in a music backup on an external drive, complete. Every word here is from those files. Nothing was rewritten, and the typos are all original.

Two things are deliberately missing: the guestbook, and the band members’ birth dates. Everything else is exactly as it was.
